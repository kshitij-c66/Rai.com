About the Club

Yaar, yeh

Chugh x Paaji is not a serious cricket club — it's an adda. A gathering of people who love the game, the banter, and believe cricket is best played with friends and absolutely no overthinking.

Born out of lazy Sunday afternoons and a group chat that spiralled into something real, we now run the Paaji League — our own tournament where every match becomes a memory and every dismissal becomes a story.

Season II is here. More teams. More chaos. Same vibes.
